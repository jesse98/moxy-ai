A few things to know.

Do I need to be online all the time?

Your AI can keep conversations going when you’re offline. You still help shape its personality, choose content and introduce it to your audience.

How does it earn?

The experience supports paid messages, selected content and tips. Your earnings depend on fan activity, fees and the revenue share agreed with Moxy.

Will fans know it’s AI?

Yes. The experience is presented as your AI, so fans understand who they’re interacting with.

What happens before launch?

You complete your creator profile and supply the content you want to use. Moxy walks you through the setup, commercial terms and launch process.
